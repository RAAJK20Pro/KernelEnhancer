### KernelEnhancer V6.0 Changelog

**Author:** @RAAJK20Pro  
**Channel:** https://t.me/KernelEnhancer

KernelEnhancer (stable-2026.11.09)

- Three profiles now: Battery, Balanced, Performance. Your old V5 tuning is the Balanced one.
- Action button cycles through the profiles and applies them right away, no reboot.
- Restore stock puts every original kernel value back, also without a reboot.
- New WebUI in the Network Enhancer style, with Light / Extreme Black themes and 15 languages.
- Home shows your profile, kernel, RAM/ZRAM usage and CPU clusters live.
- Logs viewer with search, copy and export, plus a one-tap bug report.
- Zip is signed and every file is checked on install, so a modified zip won't flash.
